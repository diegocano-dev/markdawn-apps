How it is saved

Page formatting, such as alignment or the position of each box, goes in comments that viewers do not show. The content stays as normal Markdown, in reading order.

<!-- text: x=50 y=10 w=45 h=20 -->
# Resultados del trimestre

<!-- text: x=50 y=34 w=45 h=56 -->
- Ventas +12 %
- Tres clientes nuevos
