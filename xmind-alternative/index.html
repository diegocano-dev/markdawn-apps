XMind alternative

A free XMind alternative that saves your maps as plain Markdown

MarkDawn Mind works like the mind-mapping apps you know, but every map is a plain .md file: readable in any editor, at home in Git or Obsidian, and ready for ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. It opens your XMind files too.

Why people switch from XMind

Your maps are plain text

An XMind file is a zipped package that only XMind really understands. A MarkDawn map is Markdown: headings and lists you can read, search, diff and edit anywhere, even without MarkDawn.

Made for working with AI

Paste a map into any AI and it understands it straight away. Ask your AI to write a map, open it in MarkDawn and keep working visually. No export step, no plugin.

No account, no subscription

The free version has no sign-up and no watermark. MarkDawn Mind Pro is a one-time payment. Your maps live as files on your disk, never on our servers.

Bring your XMind maps

Open .xmind files from current XMind and XMind 8 directly. Topics, notes, links, folded branches, sheets and the layout come across when there is an equivalent.

When XMind is still the better choice

If your team needs to edit the same map at the same time, or you rely on polished native phone apps, XMind is a mature option and we would rather say so. MarkDawn Mind is for people who want their maps to be files they own, readable by humans, tools and AI.

Move your XMind maps in a minute

  1. Open MarkDawn Mind in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
  2. Choose File › Open and pick your .xmind file, or drop it on the map.
  3. Check the map: topics, notes, links and sheets are kept.
  4. Save it: you now have a .md file you can open anywhere.

Questions

Is MarkDawn Mind really free?

Yes. The free version is open source (GPL v3), has no account, no ads and no watermark. MarkDawn Mind Pro adds more layouts, canvases, PDF and Word import and more styles for a one-time payment.

Can I open my existing XMind files?

Yes. MarkDawn Mind opens files from current XMind and XMind 8, plus FreeMind and Freeplane (.mm) and OPML.

Can I go back to XMind later?

Your maps are Markdown, which XMind and most mind-mapping apps can import. You are never locked in.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Install it from the browser as an app, or download the single HTML file. You only need internet to activate Pro.

Where are my maps stored?

On your device: as .md files you save, or in your browser. In Chrome and Edge you can link a work folder so maps save straight to your files.
